# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site serves Tatars, Bashkirs, families, young activists, adults, children, and interested residents in Almaty who want to understand what the Idel cultural center does, follow events, find contacts, and see real community stories.

## Product Purpose

`idel-almaty.kz` is the public website for the Tatar-Bashkir cultural center "Idel" in Almaty. It publishes center news, event announcements, activist stories, photos, contacts, and social links in Russian. Success means visitors quickly understand the center's community role, trust the information, and can move to Instagram, 2GIS, events, stories, or contacts without friction.

## Brand Personality

Community-minded, cultural, clear. The voice should feel local, human, respectful, and active rather than corporate or decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not make the site feel like a generic corporate landing page, a stock cultural festival template, or a polished but anonymous NGO brochure. Avoid replacing real center and event photos with generic imagery, overusing ornamental ethnic motifs, or redesigning away from the existing editorial/community character without an explicit request.

## Design Principles

- Lead with real community evidence: center news, activists, events, photos, and practical links should carry the experience.
- Keep navigation compact and obvious: visitors should reach events, stories, gallery, and contacts quickly.
- Preserve cultural specificity without caricature: names, places, language, and real photos matter more than decorative symbols.
- Prioritize readable Russian content: headings, metadata, cards, and long-form pages should stay clear on mobile and desktop.
- Make calls to action practical: Instagram, 2GIS, and contact paths should feel immediate and trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target solid WCAG 2.1 AA basics: readable contrast, semantic headings and landmarks, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, meaningful image alt text, and responsive layouts that do not overlap or truncate important Russian text. Respect reduced-motion preferences for any future animation.
